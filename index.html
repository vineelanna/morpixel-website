ABOUT MORPIXEL

A design lab for the space between physical and digital.

MORPIXEL DESIGNLAB (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED is a design-led digital manufacturing company focused on personalized physical products, functional décor and smart connected products.

Our approach combines reusable digital designs, standardized production workflows and customizable materials to make low-volume personalization more efficient, scalable and commercially viable.

WHAT WE BUILD

One manufacturing platform. Many product possibilities.

MORPIXEL is not limited to one product or one category. We are building a flexible design and manufacturing capability that can evolve with customer needs.

◩

Personalized Décor

Wall clocks, layered wall art, photo products, name products and personalized home décor created through digital fabrication.

◇

Signage & Branding

Custom nameplates, office signage, displays, branded merchandise and visual identity products for homes and businesses.

✦

Gifting & B2B

Personalized gifts, corporate gifting, event merchandise and small-batch branded products for teams, customers and occasions.

Smart Products

Physical products enhanced by software, connected services and AI-assisted interfaces — beginning with our MINDWALL initiative.

IN DEVELOPMENT MORPIXEL SMART PRODUCTS

MINDWALL

Your wall. Your information. Your AI.

MINDWALL is a proposed customizable ambient smart display designed to combine personalized physical design with productivity information and AI-assisted interaction.

✓

Calendar, reminders, tasks and personalized information

✓

Push-to-talk voice capture for notes and actions

✓

AI-assisted interpretation of notes, tasks and calendar intent

✓

Customizable frame, photos, artwork, colors and branding

✓

Designed as an ambient wall object, not simply a mounted tablet

MINDWALL is currently in concept / product-development stage. Features shown are proposed and subject to change during prototyping and validation.
