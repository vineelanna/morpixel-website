About Morpixel

Physical products,
designed differently.

MORPIXEL DESIGNLAB (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED is a design-led product development and digital manufacturing company focused on personalized physical products and emerging connected-product experiences. Our approach brings design, fabrication, customization and technology into one product-development workflow.

What we build

From meaningful décor
to intelligent products.

Our portfolio is designed to grow across consumer and business applications while retaining one principle: products should be capable of becoming personal without requiring conventional mass-production volumes.

01 / PERSONALIZED

Décor & Wall Products

Design-led wall décor, clocks, layered products and personalized physical pieces using digitally variable artwork and fabrication.

02 / BUSINESS

Gifting, Signage & Branding

Made-to-order corporate gifts, nameplates, signage and branded products for businesses, events and institutions.

03 / PRODUCT LAB

Connected Products

Exploration of physical products that combine customization, ambient interfaces and intelligent software — including WALLGENDA.

Technology & Manufacturing

Digital by design.
Physical by outcome.

MORPIXEL's proposed production model uses reusable digital design, laser-based fabrication, engraving, UV/digital printing, modular assembly and made-to-order workflows to support low-volume personalization efficiently.

WALLGENDA

A customizable ambient wall organizer concept designed to bring calendars, tasks, reminders, notes and personal information into one persistent physical interface.

The proposed experience combines personalized physical design with push-to-talk voice interaction and AI-assisted processing that can interpret input into structured notes, tasks, reminders or calendar actions.
