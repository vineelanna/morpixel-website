Who we are

Design for a more meaningful everyday.

MORPIXEL is a design-led digital manufacturing company building personalized physical products and connected product experiences. We work across design, materials, fabrication, printing and software to turn ideas into tangible products for homes, workspaces, brands and special moments.

What we build

One design lab. Multiple product possibilities.

01

Personalized products

Wall décor, clocks, gifting, nameplates and made-to-order products shaped around names, photographs, artwork, colours and stories.

02

Brand & business products

Signage, branded décor, corporate gifting and custom fabricated products for businesses, events and institutional requirements.

03

Smart product initiatives

Connected physical products that combine digital fabrication with useful software and intelligent interfaces. WALLGENDA is our first concept in this direction.

Technology & manufacturing

From a digital idea to a physical product.

Our approach is built around digitally variable design and standardized production workflows, enabling low-volume customization without treating every order like a completely new manufacturing process.

WALLGENDA

A customizable ambient wall organizer designed to bring calendars, tasks, reminders, notes and personal information into one persistent physical interface.

The proposed experience combines personalized physical design with push-to-talk voice interaction and AI-assisted processing that can interpret input into structured notes, tasks, reminders or calendar actions.
